How we work

A six-step delivery rhythm.

Predictable timelines, transparent milestones, and a single point of contact from kickoff to launch.

01

Discovery.

We audit your current stack, interview stakeholders, and map the problem space. You leave with a written brief.

02

Strategy.

Architecture, tech stack, and scope trade-offs are decided in a working session. No surprises downstream.

03

Design.

Wireframes, brand systems, and high-fidelity interfaces delivered in Figma with full design tokens.

04

Development.

Weekly previews on staging. Code review, tests, and CI/CD from day one. You merge into your repo.

05

Testing.

Automated E2E, accessibility audits, and performance benchmarking. Every release ships with a QA report.

06

Launch.

We deploy, monitor, and stay on-call for 30 days. Handoff includes documentation and a recorded walkthrough.
